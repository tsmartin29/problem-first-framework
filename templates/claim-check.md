Every public claim (site, ads, posts, emails, manuals, store pages) has a row. No row, no claim.

## Before publishing, answer yes to all

- [ ] Every claim above has a test we ran, on the current build.
- [ ] The comparison (if any) was run on the same independent test for everyone, not as agreement with a rival.
- [ ] No rival product names or interfaces are used without checking we may.
- [ ] The problem described is one that was reported to us or documented, with the source in the pain map. No added symptoms.
- [ ] Copy is plain full sentences or fact lists. No slogans or clipped fragments.
- [ ] The ad or card shows the real, current interface.
- [ ] Numbers and dates use one format everywhere.
- [ ] Nothing says "secure", "uncrackable" or "lossless" without a proof.
- [ ] Anything we could not test is stated as untested, or left out.
- [ ] A person who has the problem (or the founder) has read it.
